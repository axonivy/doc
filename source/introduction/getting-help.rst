.. _getting-help:

Getting Help
============

If you encounter any problems, check the following sources:

.. rubric:: Support

You can get support via mail or ticket, find more information `here <https://dev.axonivy.com/support>`_
(support may be subject to additional charges, depending on your license agreement).


.. rubric:: Axon Ivy Community

The `Axon Ivy Community <https://community.axonivy.com/>`_ (or the old 
`Axon Ivy Q&A <https://answers.axonivy.com/>`_) contains a considerable
amount of knowledge and tips related to Axon Ivy Designer and Engine.


.. rubric:: Tutorials

We have on various topics tutorials available on our website. You can find them at
`Axon Ivy Tutorials <https://www.axonivy.com/tutorials>`_.
